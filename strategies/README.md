# Strategy Ideas

Research candidates for volatility indices:

- Relative Vigor Index (RVI) confirmation and threshold studies.
- Trend-following rules with volatility-aware position sizing.
- Mean-reversion tests after statistically unusual moves.
- Multi-timeframe filters and signal agreement.
- Out-of-sample backtests with spread, slippage, and risk controls.

These are research ideas, not trading advice or live strategies. Validate assumptions and risk controls before any use.

## Relative Vigor Index (RVI) note

RVI compares a smoothed close-minus-open move with a smoothed high-minus-low range. RVI/signal-line crossovers are conventional momentum interpretations, not guarantees. Formula details and smoothing conventions vary among platforms. This repository does not currently calculate RVI or produce signals.

References: [TradingView, Relative Vigor Index (RVI)](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000502358-relative-vigor-index-rvi/) and [MetaTrader 5, Relative Vigor Index](https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/indicators/oscillators/rvi).
